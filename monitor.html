Advisor link needed

Open your monitor link from the registry sheet (R2R Monitor → My stock monitor link) once on this phone. The app then remembers it.

Reading every client's holdings…
Prices are the ones in each client's sheet (they update by themselves on weekdays, about 15–20 minutes delayed). Holdings are as of each client's last file. The % before each holding is its share of that client's portfolio. A file older than 35 days is marked old file. Paused clients are not shown. This view is for you only — don't share the link.
